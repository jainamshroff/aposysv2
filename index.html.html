APOLLO
Technology 01

Satellite-Based
Ground Monitoring

ApoSys fuses orbital imagery with localized climate datasets to detect ground deformation, subsidence, and infrastructure stress across entire corridors — without dispatching a single crew.

  • Sub-centimetre displacement detection
  • Integrated Canadian climate model overlay
  • Continuous 24/7 orbital coverage
  • Early warning for slope instability & flooding
Technology 02

Climate & Weather
Intelligence

Our integrated climate engine combines local sensor data, satellite feeds, and historical weather models to predict how extreme weather events will impact infrastructure — days before they occur.

  • Frost heave & thaw cycle prediction
  • Flood risk mapping along rail corridors
  • Precipitation & snowpack analysis
  • Climate change adaptation planning
Technology 03

Railway Predictive
Maintenance

The Autonomous Track Measurement Unit (ATMU) mounts to any locomotive and operates at full line speed — capturing geometry, subsurface, and environmental data across thousands of kilometres per session.

  • Track geometry at operational speeds
  • Subsurface GPR deformity detection
  • AI-prioritized maintenance scheduling
  • Up to 25% reduction in maintenance costs
Technology 04

LiDAR Surface
Scanning

High-density LiDAR point clouds map rail surface geometry, tunnel walls, and bridge structures at millimetre precision — detecting deformities invisible to the human eye before they become failures.

  • Millimetre-precision 3D surface mapping
  • Tunnel wall convergence monitoring
  • Bridge structural deformation tracking
  • Change detection across repeated scans
Technology 05

Digital Twin
Creation

Cloud-based analytics transform raw sensor data into high-fidelity 3D digital replicas of infrastructure assets — enabling operators to simulate, monitor, and plan from anywhere in the world.

  • Real-time synchronized infrastructure models
  • 3D HD corridor mapping & change detection
  • Scenario simulation for maintenance planning
  • Remote stakeholder access & collaboration
Technology 06

RoadSight
Vegetation AI

Computer vision and AI continuously analyse video, satellite, and sensor feeds to classify road asset conditions, detect vegetation obstructions, and automate condition reporting — eliminating manual field surveys.

  • Automated sign, pole & pavement classification
  • Vegetation obstruction detection
  • Condition severity scoring
  • Remote inspection replacing field crews
