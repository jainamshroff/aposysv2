Railway Predictive Maintenance

Autonomous track
inspection at line speed

The ATMU (Autonomous Track Measurement Unit) mounts to any locomotive, scanning track surface geometry, GPR subsurface conditions, and thermal data continuously — alerting engineers to failures before they occur.

  • Track geometry at full operational speeds
  • Ground Penetrating Radar to 2m depth
  • Subsurface void and deformation detection
  • AI-prioritised maintenance scheduling
  • Up to 25% reduction in maintenance costs

Satellite-Based Ground Monitoring

Orbital intelligence
meets ground truth

ApoSys fuses orbital SAR and multispectral imagery with climate datasets to detect ground deformation, slope instability, and infrastructure stress across entire corridors — without dispatching a single crew.

  • Sub-centimetre displacement detection
  • Integrated Canadian climate model overlay
  • Continuous 24/7 orbital coverage
  • Early warning for slope instability & flooding
  • Change detection across repeated passes

Climate & Weather Intelligence

Predict the storm
before it strikes

Our integrated climate engine combines local sensor data, satellite feeds, and historical weather models to predict how extreme weather events will impact infrastructure — days before they occur, not hours.

  • Frost heave & thaw cycle prediction
  • Flood risk mapping along rail corridors
  • Precipitation & snowpack load analysis
  • Storm tracking & real-time weather alerts
  • Climate change adaptation planning
04 / 04
LiDAR Surface Scanning

Millimetre precision
point clouds

High-density LiDAR scanning maps rail surface geometry, tunnel walls, culverts, and bridge structures at millimetre precision — detecting deformities invisible to the human eye before they become critical failures.

  • 1.2 million points per second resolution
  • ±1.5mm accuracy across full corridor
  • Tunnel wall convergence monitoring
  • Bridge structural deformation tracking
  • Color-coded anomaly classification
